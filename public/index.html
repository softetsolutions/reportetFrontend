Features Overview

Built for the Way Your Field Team Works

👥

Role-Based Access

Purpose-built views for Admin, Area Manager, Zonal Manager, and MR — each seeing exactly the data relevant to their role.

📋

Daily & Monthly Reporting

MRs submit daily visit reports and monthly sales reports directly from the app — no paperwork, no spreadsheets.

🗺

Daily Tour Plans

Plan and review daily routes for MRs and managers, so every visit day starts with a clear schedule and coverage goals.

📍

Live Employee Tracking

See field employees in real time on the map — session status, routes, and location visibility for admins and managers.

⏱

Missed Doctor Tracking

MRs and Area Managers instantly see which doctors were missed, so no visit plan falls through the cracks.

📊

Sales & Visit Insights

Headquarter-wise sales, visit reports, doctor visit reports, and call averages — broken down by MR, doctor, and HQ.

🏖

Leave Management

Apply, approve, and track leaves with configurable leave settings and leave reports built into the same admin panel and mobile app.

💼

Payroll & Payslips

Run payroll, manage holidays and settings, and let employees view payslips on mobile — without a separate HR tool.

🏪

Stockist & Masters

Manage stockists, doctors, areas, and headquarters — plus employee onboarding and notifications — all from one admin panel.
